# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, discuss technical disagreements on their merits, respect privacy, and make the project welcoming to people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of acceptable behavior include constructive review, clear technical feedback, acknowledging mistakes, and focusing discussion on the software and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing private or production data, deliberate disruption, or sustained personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not publish personal information or sensitive evidence in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the project and its community.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for this project's current maintainer model.
